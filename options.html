TextGeek

Copy text from pictures, videos and screenshots

How to use it

Click the TextGeek button, or press Alt+Shift+O, then drag a box around any text you can see on the page. The text is copied straight away and shown in a small panel where you can fix it up and copy it again.

You can also right-click a picture and choose Copy text from image.

To change the shortcut, open your browser's extension shortcuts page.

Settings Saved

Private by design

The text is read on your own computer by the Tesseract engine built into TextGeek. Pictures and text are never sent anywhere, there is no account and no tracking. TextGeek only looks at a tab when you click its button, press its shortcut or use its right-click menu.

TextGeek reads English. Other languages are not included yet.
